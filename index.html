About me

Hi! I'm Daniel*. I make cool things and hope you'll find them cool too. I like languages, translation, fonts, 2D and 3D digital art, working on my own small games and modding old existing games; all exclusively human-powered.

* My name is officially written Daniël with a diaeresis ¨ and pronounced /'da:nijɛl/ (IPA) in Dutch. In English, I think it looks and sounds better if you just use the English equivalent of the name. The diaeresis only matters to Dutch orthography, and would only cause confusion if you’re pronouncing it the English way anyway.
